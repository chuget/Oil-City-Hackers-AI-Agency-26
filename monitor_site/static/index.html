Federal contract amendment monitor

Rank contracts where amendments grew well beyond the original award, then run gate checks (AG-01 to AG-09) before stating what the open record actually supports.

What it does

Some contracts start competitive and later grow through amendments or follow-on awards. That can be normal scope change, or it can be worth a closer look. This dashboard reads open procurement records, ranks high-growth cases, and applies governance gates so reviewers do not overstate what the data shows.

Review queue

Oversight teams have limited time. The ranked list puts the largest amendment-growth patterns at the top so reviewers can start with the cases that look most unusual in the data.

Named vendors, careful claims

Departments and vendors stay in the record. A high ratio is a reason to look, not a finding of misconduct. FLAGGED means monitor the pattern. INVESTIGATED means gather amendment files and scope documents before saying more in public.

Governance gates

Nine gates (AG-01 to AG-09) set the claim level from the evidence on hand: provenance, identity, ratio thresholds, harm boundaries, and what would be needed to escalate. The output card states what you can say now and what is still missing.

Built for Agency 2026, Challenge 4 (Sole Source & Amendment Creep). The tool flags patterns for review; it does not estimate dollars saved or recovered.

Use the left panel to filter by department and procedure. Department charts in section 1 stay broad; the ranked table and drill-down follow your filters.

How to read this dashboard

Contracts scanned: total records matching your current filters.
Ratio > 25% / 100% / 300%: how large amendment growth is versus original value.
Ranked list: contracts sorted from largest growth pattern to smallest.
Drill-down: details for one selected contract and its timeline trend.
Governance card: bounded statement of what the evidence supports right now.

Important: a high ratio is a signal for review, not proof of misconduct. Legitimate scope expansion can also produce large amendments.
