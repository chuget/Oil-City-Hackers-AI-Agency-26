What this site does
This app reviews public federal contract records and highlights cases where amendment value grew substantially beyond the original award. It does not decide wrongdoing. It classifies findings as: FLAGGED (pattern needs monitoring) or INVESTIGATED (stronger corroborated signal requiring follow-up evidence).

How to read this dashboard

Contracts scanned: total records matching your current filters.
Ratio > 25% / 100% / 300%: how large amendment growth is versus original value.
Ranked list: contracts sorted from largest growth pattern to smallest.
Drill-down: details for one selected contract and its timeline trend.
Governance card: bounded statement of what the evidence supports right now.

Important: a high ratio is a signal for review, not proof of misconduct. Legitimate scope expansion can also produce large amendments.
